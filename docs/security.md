# Security

## Audits

- **Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve** — audit reports for the program used
  for token launches, bonding-curve trades, and creator-fee claims.
  [View Meteora DBC audits](https://docs.meteora.ag/resources/audits/dbc)
- **Jupiter Aggregator v6** — audit reports for the program used for swaps
  of graduated tokens.
  [View Jupiter audits](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/resources/audits)

## Instant wallet (Magic)

- **Security and compliance** — Instant trading uses an embedded wallet
  from Magic Labs, which reports SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001:2022
  certification. [View Magic Trust Center](https://trust.magic.link/)
- **Key management** — Wallet keys are split into shards and are only
  reassembled to sign inside secure hardware enclaves.
  [View Magic product security](https://docs.magic.link/home/security/product-security)

## Vulnerability disclosure

*Effective October 19, 2026*

To report a security vulnerability, email **security@seekuplift.com** with
steps to reproduce and the affected area (app, web, backend, or on-chain
program). Don't report issues publicly or through support chat, and never
include seed phrases or private keys.

[View security.txt](https://seekuplift.com/.well-known/security.txt)

**What to expect.** We aim to reply within 5 business days and will keep
you updated until the issue is fixed. Please give us up to 90 days to fix
it before disclosing it publicly.

**Scope.** The Seek Uplift Android and web apps, our backend, and
seekuplift.com.

**Testing rules.** Only test with your own accounts and wallets. Don't
access other people's data or funds, disrupt the service, or use social
engineering. We won't take legal action against good-faith research that
follows these rules.

**Protecting your wallet.** Seek Uplift will never ask for your seed
phrase or private keys, or ask you to send funds. Never share them with
anyone.
